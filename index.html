About

A Silicon Valley startup center built by proven operators.

Burlingate is a Silicon Valley startup center and venture investor focused on startups founded by Asian entrepreneurs. It is comprised of proven Silicon Valley professionals from various Asian countries.

Burlingate provides a wide range of support and investment to startups, helping them scale and succeed. With its headquarters in Silicon Valley, the firm leverages both local and international expertise to foster innovation and growth.
